# Contributor agreement plan

Before accepting external code, obtain professional review and owner approval
for a Contributor License Agreement or another contributor-rights mechanism.
It should preserve project ownership clarity, commercial licensing ability,
relicensing authority, provenance, and contributor attribution without
assuming that pull-request submission transfers copyright.

The process should define covered contributions, representations of authority,
license grants, attribution, handling of employer-owned work, withdrawal and
termination effects, and recordkeeping. This planning document is not a CLA,
is not attorney-approved, and creates no binding contribution terms.
