# Legal finalization checklist

This checklist remains open until the owner completes or explicitly waives
each item. Open items do not automatically block noncommercial development,
but no claim of professionally reviewed legal protection may be made before
professional review occurs.

[ ] Obtain professional intellectual-property attorney review of the
    PolyForm, commercial-license, trademark, contribution, and
    third-party redistribution structure.

[ ] Create an attorney-reviewed commercial license agreement.

[ ] Establish an approved commercial licensing contact and process.

[ ] Register applicable computer-program copyrights with the
    U.S. Copyright Office.

[ ] Determine whether Shadow Claw, Shadow Fang, Shadow Core,
    Shadow Evidence, logos, and slogans require separate copyright
    registrations.

[ ] Conduct professional trademark clearance searches.

[ ] File appropriate trademark applications for Shadow Claw,
    Shadow Fang, Shadow Core, Shadow Evidence, and selected logos
    or slogans.

[ ] Adopt an attorney-reviewed Contributor License Agreement or
    other contributor-rights process before accepting external code.

[ ] Review privacy, data-processing, export-control, and sector-specific
    requirements before production distribution of Shadow Fang.

[ ] Review the final commercial release before changing any ™ symbol
    to ®.

[ ] Review all third-party redistributions and notice obligations for
    every final platform package.

Surface this checklist to the owner before final Shadow Claw, Shadow Fang, or
Shadow Core production releases and at overall project completion.

Legal finalization reminder:

Before considering the Shadow ecosystem fully finalized, obtain
professional legal review, register applicable copyrights, and pursue
trademark filings for Shadow Claw™, Shadow Fang™, Shadow Core™, and
related branding.
