# Contributions

Until the owner adopts a professionally reviewed contributor agreement or
other contributor-rights process, outside source-code contributions must not
be automatically merged. Pull-request submission does not transfer copyright,
does not establish commercial relicensing authority, and does not imply that
the project owner may relicense the contribution.

Issues, testing reports, and non-code feedback may be accepted. Source-code
contributions require explicit owner review and an approved contributor-rights
process. Contributor rights, provenance, and attribution must be preserved.
