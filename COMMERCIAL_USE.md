# Commercial use

The public repository does not grant commercial-use rights. Commercial use
requires a separate written commercial license signed or expressly approved by
Kache Flanery. This policy describes a contact pathway; it is not itself a
commercial license agreement, and submitting a request does not grant
permission.

Examples requiring a separate written commercial license include use by a
for-profit business; internal enterprise deployment; revenue-generating
operations; paid consulting; managed security services; use by an MSP, MSSP,
or similar provider; resale; paid redistribution; OEM bundling; incorporation
into a paid product; SaaS or hosted-service use; paid support; paid training
built around the software; commercial modifications or derivative products;
and commercial integration into another application.

Until a dedicated commercial contact is approved, direct requests to the
repository owner through the GitHub repository's approved contact mechanism.
No pricing, payment, warranty, support, indemnification, or service-level terms
are offered by this policy.
